# Cursed Bear audio credits

Field recording derivatives used in this encounter:

- U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service, **Bear Growls**, packaged by AntumDeluge under CC0 1.0. The clips were edited, pitch-shifted, and layered for the growls and roar. [Source and license](https://opengameart.org/content/bear-growls).
- Shizhao, **Bear growl.ogg** (2007), Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported. The recording was excerpted and pitch-shifted for the breath variants and blended into the roar. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bear_growl.ogg), [license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

**Original Soundtrack & Music Direction — Logachandran Hirukshanan.** The suspense bed, battle loop, Rage stem, and musical accents were created for Veil of Vera.

The awakening, combat and Rage roars use separate edits of the USFWS recordings. The claw strikes, bite, charge and slam use original mixes of those recordings and the project's CC0 Kenney Impact Sounds and RPG Audio foley.
