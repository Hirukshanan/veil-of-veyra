The author describes this as their own voice with EQ, not a real boar recording. Warning/charge/hurt/death are short pitched/layered fantasy derivatives of that single vocal; they are not four independently recorded performances.
- **Kenney � Impact Sounds**: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds . CC0, original license preserved alongside this file. Grass/concrete footsteps supply the dry cloven-hoof approximation; soft/punch impacts supply body contacts.

`audio_manifest.json` records exact original hashes, individual source filenames, pitch/gain/offset recipes, output hashes, duration, size and decoded peak. Delivery is mono 24 kHz 16-bit PCM WAV (decoded from local quality-4 Vorbis intermediates), with 10 ms fades and fixed peak headroom. No looping music, voice service or generative audio service is involved. Only the selected small derivatives ship; complete source packs remain in the ignored local validation directory.
